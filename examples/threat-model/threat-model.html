1Executive brief

This review examined 7 components and 6 data paths (4 of them crossing a trust boundary) of Acme Scheduler version 0.1.0, assessed on 2026-10-29. It identified 3 threats: 2 already have a control in place and 1 need action, none of them high priority. 42 required assessments are still missing; the coverage matrix in Part B lists them.

The design covers the main entry points well; the open items concern the job queue and operational hardening.

6How to read this report

The six threat categories (STRIDE)

Spoofing (S)
pretending to be another user or system
Tampering (T)
changing data, code or messages without permission
Repudiation (R)
denying an action because there is no trustworthy record of it
Information disclosure (I)
exposing data to people who should not see it
Denial of service (D)
making the service slow or unavailable
Elevation of privilege (E)
gaining rights or access one should not have

How risk is scored

Score = likelihood × impact
Both on a 1–5 scale: likelihood is how plausible the attack is within a year given today's controls; impact is the worst credible outcome for the business.
Severity bands
Critical 20–25 High 12–19 Medium 6–11 Low 1–5
High priority
An open threat rated High or Critical. A Critical or High risk can only be accepted with a named approver.

Status of a threat

○Open
needs action: no adequate control yet
✎Accepted
residual risk signed off by a named approver
⇄Transferred
handled by a provider or a contract
✓Mitigated
a control is designed and in place

Terms used in this report

Component
A part of the system: a kind of user or outside party, a running service, or a place data is kept.
Data path
Data moving from one component to another.
Trust boundary
A line where the level of trust changes, such as the public internet meeting our servers. Most threats sit on paths that cross one.
Coverage
Every component and data path was asked every applicable STRIDE question; a cell is either a threat, a reasoned “no threat”, or a gap.
Divergence
A place where two sources disagree (one document against another, or a document against the code). Each is a finding to resolve.

Out of scope

  • Workers have no outbound internet access except the SMTP relay.

17Method and metadata

STRIDE applied per element of the Level 1 data-flow diagram: external entities are asked about Spoofing and Repudiation; processes about all six categories; data stores about Tampering, Information disclosure and Denial of service (plus Repudiation when they hold audit data); flows about Tampering, Information disclosure and Denial of service. Risk = likelihood × impact on 1–5 scales; bands Critical 20–25, High 12–19, Medium 6–11, Low 1–5. A Critical or High threat may be accepted only with a named approver. Explicit no-threat decisions recorded: 1.
